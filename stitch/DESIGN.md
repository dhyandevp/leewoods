## Brand & Style
Lee Wood Interior is a high-end architectural interior design practice. The aesthetic is anchored in **Warm Architectural Minimalism**, **Tropical Modernism**, and **Quiet Luxury**.

### Brand Rules
1. The logo must ONLY read: **LEE WOOD INTERIOR**. Never attach "Kannur / Kerala" or any location/discipline subtitle to the logo.
2. The location ("Kannur, Kerala") belongs in the studio section, contact page, or global footer location slot.
3. Typography is calm, restrained, and editorial. Mostly regular weights with selective bronze highlights.

## Color System
- **Warm Ivory (#F3F0E8):** Main architectural canvas.
- **Deep Forest Green (#062018):** Primary text and monolithic dark sections.
- **Muted Bronze (#B98A4A):** Restrained accent for eyebrows, key accent words, and critical actions.
- **Stone Green (#68716C):** Secondary text and muted labels.
- **Border:** `rgba(6, 32, 24, 0.16)` on light, `rgba(243, 240, 232, 0.16)` on dark.

## Grid & Rhythm
A strict 12-column global grid (`max-width: 1480px`, `calc(100% - 80px)` desktop) ensures all sections align to the same horizontal boundaries. Alternating image/text compositions provide visual rhythm without repetitive card grids.
